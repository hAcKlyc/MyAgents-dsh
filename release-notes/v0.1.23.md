# MyAgents-dsh 0.1.23

WebSearch keeps completed query results when other queries fail.

- Merge successful queries through the existing source limits and include bounded explanations for failed queries, without cancelling successful siblings.
- Forward validated Host answers and verification warnings through native search output and replay metadata, so partial failures retain their useful context.
- Keep all-failed batches as tool errors and distinguish them from completed searches with no hits.
- Preserve caller cancellation and Product deadlines: drain pending searches and stop before publishing late partial results.
